About


I am passionate about automation, problem-solving, and independent research. My main interests include physics, electronics, electrical engineering, and developing innovative solutions to complex problems. I particularly enjoy troubleshooting circuit errors, exploring unfamiliar subjects, and understanding how things work beyond the limits of traditional education.

Gaming is another major interest of mine. I enjoy a wide range of genres, from simulation and automation games such as Factorio to action and open-world adventures. Some of my favorite games include Half-Life 2, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, Grand Theft Auto IV, Pokémon Red and Blue, classic Nintendo 64 Mario games, and the Tony Hawk's Pro Skater series.

I also have a strong interest in nonfiction, philosophy, psychology, and darker literary themes. Some of the works that have influenced me include The Psychology of Money, Fahrenheit 451, The Art of War, and the novels of Fyodor Dostoevsky. I especially appreciate Russian and German literature for its depth, psychological insight, and exploration of the darker aspects of human nature.

Research and the pursuit of knowledge are central to my ambitions. I am motivated by the challenge of studying unfamiliar subjects and turning complex ideas into meaningful understanding. Although traditional academic education has sometimes been difficult for me, my curiosity and desire to learn continue to push me forward. I hope to keep developing my skills, pursue independent research, and eventually build a future based on discovery, creativity, and problem-solving.
